# Odometry-guided localization experiments

These experiments use an opt-in G2 search node with Quickstart on Boreas.
The mapping session is `boreas-2020-11-26-13-58` and the query session
is `boreas-2021-09-02-11-42`. The 1 km square comes from the mapping
session. The bags replay LiDAR clouds and 5 Hz relative odometry
generated by KISS-ICP at 1×. The reference trajectory sets the initial
offset and provides the ground truth for evaluation.

## Moving startup and tracking after a scan gap

The follow-up used the 1× Boreas bags and KISS-ICP TF. The experimental
`odom_window_g2.py` replaced the stock G2 node in a test launch. It uses the
odom transform at the scan timestamp to move a 100 m square occupancy window.
The three `prior_odom_*` parameters place the origin and heading of the odom
frame in the map. In the replay they were set from the reference start pose
with a 10 m eastward and a 5° heading offset. No absolute reference pose was
updated during playback.

The comparison changed several settings together: cached whole-map NDT
scoring, 35 m scoring range, eight BBS candidates, and reliable cloud
delivery for the localizer. The startup manager still checked agreement
and verified the pose against later scans.

| Site | Stock fixed-window startup | Experimental startup | Reset time in bag | Reset position error |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| 4010 | Unverified | Verified | 9.51 s | 0.91 m |
| 4296 | Unverified | Verified | 7.01 s | 0.29 m |

Successful-query runtimes were 1.3–2.1 s across the six answered queries.
Site 4296 tracked to the end of the 36 s bag, with 1.43 m final position
error. Site 4010 stopped matching near 15 s as the road approached and
crossed the west edge of the clipped map.

For the separate 4010 gap test, the initial pose was given and the first scan
was held for 4 s before the 1× segment. The west margin added 234,942 points
from the mapping session, with up to 75 m of additional coverage. Both runs
used identical localizer parameters and a cloud-only gap from 8 to 11 s.

| Map | First accepted match after the gap | Position error at that match | Last accepted match | Accepted matches after the gap |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Original 1 km square | 11.41 s | 0.99 m | 14.93 s | 18 |
| With west margin | 11.41 s | 0.99 m | 35.88 s | 119 |

With the margin, the final position error was 0.22 m at 35.88 s. The
margin used new scans from the mapping session outside the clipped west
boundary.

The combined test at site 4296 used the original map. It initialized while
moving, with one reset at 5.01 s and 0.93 m error. Omitting 15 clouds from
18 to 21 s kept the odometry stream running. The first accepted match after
the gap was at 21.36 s with 1.46 m error. There were 72 accepted matches
after the gap, including the final scan at 36.09 s with 1.43 m error.
G3 was disabled for these gaps, so this measures tracking reacquisition
with continuous odometry. Repeat runs and hardware validation are still
needed.

## Fixed versus moving windows with identical scoring

Set `refresh_window:=false` on the experimental G2 node to keep the 100 m
square centred on the startup prior. The default moves the centre using the
odometry transform at the scan timestamp. Both settings require that
transform and use the same scan-time motion compensation.

The comparison used Boreas map session `boreas-2020-11-26-13-58` and
query session `boreas-2021-09-02-11-42`. Each run replayed the scans and
5 Hz LiDAR-derived KISS-ICP odometry at 1× against the same 1 km square.
Both modes used C++ BBS with 5° bins, 256 points and eight candidates, cached
whole-map NDT refinement with a 35 m scan range and 0.4 m target voxels, and
reliable delivery to the tracking node. The startup fitness shortcut
threshold was 0.0001 so the observed startups used agreement between
queries. The prior offset was applied to map X and yaw at the first scan.
Site IDs are zero-based scan indices in the query trajectory CSV. Site
7400 was chosen before searching based on route coverage and was held
out from the earlier runs.

Startup time is the bag time when Quickstart reported `active` with source
`global`. The position errors are from the first tracking pose at or after
that status.

| Site | Prior offset | Fixed startup time | Moving startup time | Fixed position error | Moving position error |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 4010 | +10 m, +5° | 7.01 s | 6.51 s | 0.17 m | 0.15 m |
| 4296 | +10 m, +5° | 7.76 s | 7.51 s | 0.18 m | 0.16 m |
| 4296 | −10 m, −5° | 7.76 s | 7.76 s | 0.18 m | 0.18 m |
| 7400 | +10 m, +5° | 6.51 s | 6.51 s | 0.92 m | 0.92 m |

Each mode verified startup in all four cases. The small timing differences
come from single runs and do not establish a speed advantage. At the held-out
site, both modes tracked the full 336 m replay and finished at 36.09 s with
1.45 m position error. The site 4010 runs both stopped accepting matches at
14.93 s near the west map boundary.

An additional moving-window run at site 4537 with a +10 m, +5° prior
verified startup at 15.51 s with 1.24 m position error and tracked to
36.09 s with 1.17 m error.

## Simultaneous LiDAR and odometry interruption

Both runs used site 4296 and initialized while moving. The replay dropped
15 clouds and their 15 odometry transforms from 18 to 21 s. The second run
also translated the resumed odometry to a zero origin while preserving
its orientation and subsequent relative motion. G3 was enabled with the
preset's 0.95 reinitialization threshold in both runs.

| Odometry on resume | First accepted match | Position error at first match | Accepted matches after the gap | G3 search |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Continuous frame | 21.36 s | 1.46 m | 72 | No query |
| Origin reset | — | — | 0 | Queried G2 and rejected the candidate |

With a continuous frame, the first match came 0.21 s after the first
returning scan, and the run finished with 1.43 m position error at 36.09 s.
With an origin reset, the first odometry-based registration seed was 140 m
from the last accepted pose. NDT rejected all 73 scans after the gap. The
reinitialization score was 0.65 at the end of the bag, below the 0.95
threshold for the localizer's `/reinitialization_requested` topic. The G3
supervisor independently queried G2 after the odometry dropout and rejected
the weak candidate. The reset moved the experimental search window back
near its startup prior, about 169 m from the first returning scan in the
map. Recovery from an odometry restart needs detection of the frame
discontinuity and a search window independent of the stale odometry origin.

## Restarting KISS-ICP on the Boreas input

KISS-ICP 1.3.0 processed the same 175 raw scans from site 4296 at about
5 Hz in three runs. The uninterrupted run processed all scans. The
input-interruption run omitted the 15 scans between 18 and 21 s and kept
the estimator state. The restart run omitted the same scans and started a
fresh estimator process on the first returning scan. All runs used the
same 1–60 m range and 0.6 m map voxel size. The Boreas reference was used
to measure displacement between the gap endpoints. [Boreas dataset](https://www.boreas.utias.utoronto.ca/)

From the last scan before the gap at 17.84 s to the first returning scan
at 21.16 s, the reference positions were 40.77 m apart. Uninterrupted
KISS-ICP estimated a 42.33 m displacement. The estimator that kept its
state across the interruption estimated 1.99 m and was 40.34 m from the
uninterrupted pose at the first returning scan.

The fresh process reported zero translation and identity rotation on its
first scan. Relative to the last pose from the previous process, this
was a 140.01 m position discontinuity and an 11.56° rotation. At the same
scan timestamp it was 181.11 m from the uninterrupted estimate. The
upstream ROS 2 wrapper constructs the estimator on startup and publishes
its pose as odometry, with TF enabled by parameter. It also exposes
`kiss/reset` to clear the estimator state. [KISS-ICP ROS 2 wrapper](https://github.com/PRBonn/kiss-icp/blob/v1.3.0/ros/src/OdometryServer.cpp)

Quickstart replayed the interrupted and restarted runs at 1× using their
measured odometry and deskewed clouds. Both triggered the G3 supervisor's
odometry dropout fallback and neither asserted the localizer's
`/reinitialization_requested` topic.

- With the estimator state preserved, G3 published a reset with 1.61 m
  position error. The first healthy match occurred at 31.32 s, 10.16 s
  after scans resumed. The run finished at 36.09 s with 1.42 m position
  error. The supervisor's final cross-check was still pending at replay
  shutdown.
- With the estimator restarted, the 100 m search window was centred
  161 m from the reference pose at the scan used by the recovery query.
  G3 rejected the weak candidate, and all 73 post-gap scans failed
  tracking.

## Search after an odometry discontinuity

Set `enable_restart_recovery:=true` on `odom_window_g2.py` with
`refresh_window:=true`. The experiment detects a horizontal odometry
jump larger than 10 m plus 30 m/s times the interval between the
odometry samples. It uses same-stamp `pcl_pose` and healthy
`alignment_status` messages to remember the latest confirmed map pose.

After a jump, G2 projects motion from the first odometry sample in the
new frame onto the last healthy map pose. It widens the search window
from 100 m to 150 m square at the default radius to allow for movement
during the outage. A healthy scan match re-establishes the map alignment
of the new odometry frame and restores the original window size. If
there is no healthy pose yet, G2 searches the full map. The G3 candidate
selection and verification policy still applies.

| Site 4296 replay | Odometry jump detected | Healthy matches after the gap | Last healthy match |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| KISS-ICP process restart | Yes | 16 | 35.88 s, 1.30 m position error |
| Replay with a translated odometry origin | Yes | 22 | 36.09 s, 1.43 m position error |
| Three-second gap with continuous odometry | No | 72 | 36.09 s, 1.43 m position error |

The KISS-ICP restart run first matched after recovery at 32.77 s with
1.43 m position error, 11.62 s after the first returning scan. A repeat
reached its first healthy match at 30.90 s, 9.75 s after scans resumed.
G3 completed the cross-check and confirmed recovery. The repeat tracked
to the final scan at 36.09 s with 1.41 m position error. The
continuous-odometry run recovered without a new pose reset.

In a separate run, pausing KISS-ICP input while retaining estimator
state did not trigger a jump. G3 found a candidate with 1.60 m position
error. Tracking did not recover before the end of that replay. The
preceding run in this note recovered from the same input pause.
